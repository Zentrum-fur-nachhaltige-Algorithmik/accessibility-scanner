Laptop Return Policy

Return Conditions

Laptops purchased from our store may be returned within 15 days of the original purchase date, subject to the following conditions:

  • The laptop must be in its original packaging with all accessories included.
  • Software must not have been activated or registered.
  • A restocking fee of 15% applies to opened items.
  • Custom-configured laptops are not eligible for return.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log in to your account and navigate to Order History.
  2. Select the laptop order you wish to return.
  3. Click "Request Return" and follow the prompts.
  4. Print the prepaid shipping label provided.
