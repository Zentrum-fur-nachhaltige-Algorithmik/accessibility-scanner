Missing and Generic Page Titles (BAD)

This page itself has an empty title element (Violation 1). Below are illustrations of additional title failures.

Violation 1: Empty Title Element

This page's <title> element is present but empty. The browser tab shows no meaningful text. Screen readers announce nothing or a URL fragment when switching to this tab.
  localhost:3000/account/settings

This is the account settings page, but users have no way to identify it from the title alone.

Violation 2: Generic Non-Descriptive Titles

Pages across the site use generic titles like "Untitled", "Page", or "Home" that do not describe the specific content. Users cannot distinguish between tabs or bookmarks.

These pages all exist in the same site but their titles are meaningless:

Untitled Document example.com/products/laptops

Laptop product catalog with 200+ models and comparison tools.

Page example.com/checkout/shipping

Shipping address form for order checkout.

Untitled example.com/account/order-history

Complete order history with tracking information.

Violation 3: Identical Titles Across Different Pages

Every page on the site uses the same title "MyStore". Users with multiple tabs open cannot distinguish between pages. Screen reader users navigating by window list hear the same name repeated.

All pages share the identical title “MyStore”:

MyStore mystore.com/

Homepage with featured products and seasonal deals.

MyStore mystore.com/cart

Shopping cart with 3 items totalling $149.97.

MyStore mystore.com/account/profile

User profile settings and preferences.

MyStore mystore.com/support/tickets

Support ticket history and new ticket form.
