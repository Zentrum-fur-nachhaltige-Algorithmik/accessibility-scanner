How does SecureShield Enterprise protect against ransomware?

SecureShield Enterprise uses multiple layers of protection against ransomware:

  • Behavioral Analysis: AI monitors file system behavior to detect encryption activities
  • Real-time Blocking: Automatically quarantines suspicious processes and files
  • Backup Protection: Immutable backups that can't be encrypted by ransomware
  • Network Segmentation: Prevents lateral movement across your network
  • Email Security: Advanced phishing protection to prevent initial infection
  • Incident Response: 24/7 security team ready to respond to active threats

Our platform has a 99.8% success rate in preventing ransomware attacks from causing data loss.
