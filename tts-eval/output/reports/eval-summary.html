Key Findings

  • temml handles 97.6% of ground truth formulas directly — sufficient for tiers 1-2.
  • latex2mathml fallback captures an additional 34 formulas that temml misses.
  • Hungarian/greedy matching significantly outperforms sequential position matching for formula alignment.
  • Mistral OCR formula recall varies: excellent for formula-dense papers, lower for papers with many trivial single-variable formulas.
  • Most conversion failures stem from OCR delimiter quirks (\Big{(} vs \Big() and unsupported packages (\xymatrix).

Recommendations

  • Add pre-processing to fix common OCR delimiter patterns before conversion.
  • Use Hungarian/greedy matching for formula alignment in quality evaluation.
  • Consider LaTeXML as a third fallback level for the remaining ~0.5% failure cases.
  • Filter trivial single-variable formulas from recall metrics for a more meaningful assessment.
