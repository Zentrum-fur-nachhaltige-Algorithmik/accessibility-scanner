This audio module plays narration over continuous ambient office sounds (typing, phones ringing, chatter). There is no separate volume control or toggle for the background audio. Users must listen to both tracks together.

Missing: Separate background audio toggle or volume control

Violation 4: Podcast with continuous background soundscape

[Audio Player: True Crime Podcast — Episode 12 — 40 minutes]

This podcast uses dramatic background music and sound effects throughout the entire episode. The background audio varies in volume and sometimes nearly matches the speech volume during dramatic moments. No speech-only version is available.

Peak background level: -8 dB (during dramatic moments)

Average speech level: -10 dB

Background audio exceeds speech at multiple points in the episode.
