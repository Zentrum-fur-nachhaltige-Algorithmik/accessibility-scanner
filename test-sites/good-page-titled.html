Descriptive Page Titles (GOOD)

This page demonstrates WCAG 2.4.2 compliance. Its own title is "Account Settings - Manage Profile and Preferences | MyStore", which is descriptive, unique, and identifies both the page content and the site.

Pattern 1: Unique Descriptive Titles Across a Site

Each page in the site has a unique title that describes its specific content, followed by the site name:

Featured Products and Deals | MyStore mystore.com/

Homepage with featured products and seasonal deals.

Shopping Cart (3 items) | MyStore mystore.com/cart

Shopping cart displaying 3 items totalling $149.97.

Shipping Address - Checkout Step 2 of 4 | MyStore mystore.com/checkout/shipping

Shipping address form, the second step in the checkout process.

Order History - View Past Orders | MyStore mystore.com/account/order-history

Complete order history with tracking information.

Pattern 2: Titles Reflect Dynamic State

Page titles update to reflect the current content or state, so users always know what they are viewing:

Search Results for "wireless headphones" (42 found) | MyStore mystore.com/search?q=wireless+headphones

Search results showing 42 wireless headphone products with filters.

Sony WH-1000XM5 - Product Details | MyStore mystore.com/products/sony-wh-1000xm5

Product detail page with specifications, reviews, and purchase options.

(2) New Messages - Support Tickets | MyStore mystore.com/support/tickets

Support tickets with unread message count shown in the title.
