Accessible Instructions with Multiple Cues (GOOD)

This page demonstrates WCAG 1.3.3 compliance: instructions never rely solely on sensory characteristics.

Pattern 1: Buttons Identified by Name and Visual Cue

To submit your application, click the "Submit" button (round, green). To go back without saving, click the "Cancel" button (gray, rectangular).

Pattern 2: Form Validation with Text, Icon, and Color

Fields marked with a warning icon and "Error" label need correction. A checkmark and "Valid" label confirm accepted input.


Error: Full name is required.

Valid: Email address accepted.

Pattern 3: Navigation References Include Element Names

To manage your account, use the "Account Settings" link in the navigation menu. For quick actions, open the "Quick Actions" panel. Your profile summary appears in the "Profile" section at the top of the dashboard.
