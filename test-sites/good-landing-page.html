Example Research Institute

An independent research organisation for accessible science.

The institute studies the mathematical and technological foundations of accessible systems, documents the contributions of researchers with disabilities and works towards structural accessibility in the research system.

About the institute

Mission

The institute treats accessibility as a basic principle of research rather than as a technical add-on. Research that is not accessible excludes perspectives that would enrich it.

Tasks and goals

Work is organised in four areas: foundational research, accessible presentation of scientific content, history of science, and science policy. Each area publishes working papers, position statements and shorter research notes.

Research areas

Foundations of assistive technology

Formal models behind navigation aids, adaptive learning systems and the conditions under which assistive systems can offer reliable guarantees.

Accessible scientific notation

Modality-independent presentation of formulas, diagrams and models, including braille codes, MathML and sonification.

History of science

A biographical archive documenting the contributions of researchers with disabilities and placing them in their historical context.

Science policy

Analyses and recommendations on accessibility in universities, publishing and funding institutions.
