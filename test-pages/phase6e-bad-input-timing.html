⚠️ Phase 6E Bad Example - ACCESSIBILITY VIOLATIONS

WARNING: This page intentionally contains multiple WCAG violations for testing purposes. Do not use as implementation guidance.

❌ 2.5.1 Pointer Gestures - VIOLATIONS

Complex Gestures Without Alternatives

Violation: Requires multi-point gestures with no simple alternative.

Three-finger tap or complex mouse gesture required
Drag Me
Drop Zone

Problem: No keyboard or simple click alternatives provided for complex drag and multi-touch gestures.

❌ 2.5.2 Pointer Cancellation - VIOLATIONS

Immediate Actions Without Cancellation

Violation: Actions execute immediately on down-event without cancellation option.

Problem: Critical actions trigger on mouse down with no way to cancel if user changes mind.

❌ 2.5.3 Label in Name - VIOLATIONS

Mismatched Labels and Names

Problem: Accessible names don't match visible labels, confusing voice control users.

❌ 2.5.4 Motion Actuation - VIOLATIONS

Motion-Only Controls

Violation: Shake device to refresh - no alternative method or disable option.

Content will refresh when you shake your device

No button alternative provided

No way to disable motion detection

❌ 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable - VIOLATIONS

Form with Hidden Timeout

Violation: 2-minute timeout with no warning or extension option.

Time remaining: 2:00

Problems:

  • No visible timeout indicator
  • No warning before expiry
  • No way to extend time
  • Data lost on timeout

❌ 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide - VIOLATIONS

Auto-Playing Content Without Controls

Violation: Content starts automatically with no user controls.

🎵 Auto-playing audio content...
No pause, stop, or volume controls provided
Constantly Moving Content - No Way to Stop
⚠️ FLASHING CONTENT - SEIZURE RISK

Problems:

  • Auto-play starts immediately on load
  • No pause or stop controls
  • No way to hide distracting content
  • Dangerous flashing rate (>3 Hz)

❌ 2.2.6 Timeouts - VIOLATIONS

Data Loss on Timeout

Violation: User data is lost when session expires without warning.

Session expires silently in: 1:30

Problems:

  • No advance warning of timeout
  • No data preservation
  • No recovery mechanism
  • Silent failure on submit
