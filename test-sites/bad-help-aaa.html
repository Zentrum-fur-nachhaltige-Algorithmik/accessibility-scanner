Bad Help (3.3.5 AAA)

Violation 1: Tax form with no help text or instructions

No help text, tooltips, or documentation links are provided. Users must already know tax terminology and filing codes.

Violation 2: Unhelpful error messages with no corrective guidance
Error: Invalid input.
Error: Validation failed.
Error: Bad value.

Error messages say "Invalid input," "Validation failed," and "Bad value" without explaining what is wrong or how to fix it.

Violation 3: Medical form with no contextual help

Users are expected to know medical billing codes (ICD-10, NDC, CPT, NPI) with no explanations, examples, or lookup tools provided.

Violation 4: Application form with no help resources

No link to documentation, no help button, no contact information, no FAQ. Users have no way to get assistance.
