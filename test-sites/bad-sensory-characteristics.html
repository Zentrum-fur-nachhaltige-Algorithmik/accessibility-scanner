Sensory-Only Instructions (BAD)

This page demonstrates violations of WCAG 1.3.3: instructions that rely solely on sensory characteristics.

Violation 1: Shape-Only References

Instructions reference buttons only by their shape. Users who cannot see the shapes have no way to identify the correct control.

To submit your application, click the round button. To cancel, press the square button.

If you see a triangle icon, it means there is a warning. Review the section marked with the triangle before proceeding.

Violation 2: Color-Only References

Error and success states are communicated only through text color. Users who cannot perceive color differences will miss these instructions.

Please review the form below. The red text indicates required fields that have errors. Green text means the field is valid.

Violation 4: Size-Only References

Instructions reference elements only by their visual size. This is meaningless to screen reader users.

Click the larger button to proceed to checkout. The smaller button saves your cart for later.

Important notices are shown in large text. Optional details appear in smaller text. Read the large text first.
