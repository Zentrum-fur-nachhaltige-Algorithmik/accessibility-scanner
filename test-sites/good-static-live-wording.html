Where you live and how we update this guidance

The rules are different depending on where you live. If you live in Northern Ireland you must bring your insurance certificate with you.

How this page is kept up to date

We refresh this guidance whenever the law changes. Editors have a checklist for updating each section, and every change is recorded so you can see what was amended and when.

Other ways to follow the guidance

You can subscribe to the email feed for this topic, or read the news ticker archive of past announcements. Neither of them changes anything on this page: nothing here moves, blinks, scrolls or reloads on its own, so there is nothing for a pause or stop control to act on.

Live services

The live application service opens at 7am. Once you start an application the page shows a static summary of what you entered; it does not poll the server or rewrite itself while you read it.
