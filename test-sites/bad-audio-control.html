Missing Audio Controls (BAD)

This page demonstrates violations of WCAG 1.4.2: audio plays automatically without user control mechanisms.

Violation 1: Auto-Playing Background Music Without Controls

An audio element auto-plays without the controls attribute. Users have no way to pause, stop, or adjust volume. This interferes with screen readers.
